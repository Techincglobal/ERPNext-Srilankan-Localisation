# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 30 September 2026**

This policy describes the privacy practices of the ERPNext Sri Lankan Localisation app (the “App”), maintained by TechInc Global. It applies to the App itself. It does not replace the privacy policy of the organization operating your Frappe or ERPNext site, or the terms of your hosting provider.

## Information the App handles

The App adds Sri Lankan tax and registration fields to ERPNext records, including Company, Customer, Supplier, and Sales Invoice records. Depending on how a site is configured, these records may contain business contact details, addresses, and identifiers such as TIN, VAT registration numbers, SSCL registration numbers, and BRC numbers. The App also uses transaction and tax information to provide tax templates, reports, invoice formatting, and naming-series behavior.

The App handles this information within the Frappe site where it is installed and stores it in that site's ERPNext database. The App does not operate a hosted service for this information. The App's code does not include analytics or telemetry, and it does not intentionally transmit site records to TechInc Global.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide the App's features: configuring Sri Lankan tax records, displaying tax details on invoices, producing tax schedules, and generating and maintaining document numbers. Site administrators control which records are entered and who can access them.

## Storage, retention, and security

Site records, database backups, and server logs are managed by the organization operating the site and its hosting provider. Their access controls, retention periods, backup practices, and security measures depend on that environment. Contact your site administrator for requests about information stored on a particular site.

## Third-party services

The App does not send ERPNext records to an external analytics or advertising service. Frappe, ERPNext, the hosting provider, and any integrations configured by a site administrator may have their own data handling practices. Those services are outside this policy's scope.

## Questions and updates

For questions about the App's privacy practices, contact [servers@techincglobal.com](mailto:servers@techincglobal.com). For questions about records held by an ERPNext site, contact that site's administrator. We may update this policy when the App or its practices change; the effective date above will be revised when that happens.
